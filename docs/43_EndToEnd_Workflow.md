# TZAHU CRM: Complete Sales Workflow

> **For:** Sales team, founders, non-technical stakeholders  
> **What this covers:** How a customer moves through our CRM from first contact to loyal customer

---

## The Big Picture

Think of TZAHU CRM as your entire sales team working 24/7. It tracks every customer interaction, automates repetitive tasks, and helps you close more deals faster.

```
Lead → Qualified Lead → Customer → Happy Customer → Loyal Customer
  (stranger)   (interested)   (bought)    (helped)     (stays + refers)
```

---

## Step 1: Getting New Leads (Marketing & Inbound)

**What happens:** Someone shows interest in your product.

**Where leads come from:**
- Someone fills out a "Contact Us" form on your website
- They click an ad on Google/LinkedIn/Facebook
- They attend a webinar you hosted
- A partner refers them
- You manually add them from a business card
- You import a list from an Excel file

**What the CRM does automatically:**
- Creates a Lead record with their name, email, phone, company
- Checks if they already exist (no duplicates!)
- Assigns them to the right sales rep based on territory or skill
- Sends a notification: "New lead assigned to you!"
- Logs everything in the activity timeline

**What you see:**
- A list of all leads with scores (Hot/Warm/Cold)
- Who each lead is assigned to
- Where they came from (website, ad, referral)

---

## Step 2: Qualifying the Lead

**What happens:** Your sales rep contacts the lead to see if they're a real opportunity.

**The sales rep:**
- Calls the lead (call logged automatically)
- Sends an email from inside the CRM
- Notes what the lead is interested in

**What the CRM does:**
- Tracks every call, email, and meeting on the lead's timeline
- Scores the lead based on engagement (did they open the email? visit your pricing page?)
- Creates follow-up tasks: "Call back Friday at 2pm"
- Shows the lead's score: Hot (ready to buy), Warm (interested), Cold (not now)

**Decision point:**
- **Qualified** → Lead moves to the next stage
- **Disqualified** → Lead marked with reason (budget too low, wrong industry)

---

## Step 3: Converting to a Customer Record

**What happens:** The lead is interested — time to create proper customer records.

**The CRM creates three records automatically:**
1. **Contact** — The person (name, email, phone, job title)
2. **Account** — Their company (name, industry, size, website)
3. **Opportunity** — The potential deal (estimated value, expected close date)

**Think of it like this:**
- Lead = "someone who might buy" → Contact = "customer we know" + Account = "company they work for" + Opportunity = "deal we're working on"

**What happens next:**
- Welcome email sent automatically
- Task created: "Prepare demo for new contact"
- Notification sent to account manager

---

## Step 4: Moving Through the Sales Pipeline

**What happens:** The deal moves through stages toward closing.

**Typical pipeline stages:**
```
Prospecting → Qualification → Proposal → Negotiation → Closed Won
```

**At each stage, the sales rep:**
- Logs calls, emails, and meetings
- Updates the deal value and close date
- Moves the opportunity to the next stage (like dragging a card)

**Quote & Approval:**
- When the deal reaches "Proposal" stage, the rep creates a Quote
- Quote lists products, quantities, prices, discounts
- If the quote is over $10,000, it needs manager approval
- Manager gets a notification → Approves or rejects

**Forecasting:**
- The CRM shows: "You have $500,000 in pipeline, expected to close $200,000 this quarter"
- Managers can see exactly what each rep is working on

**Decision point:**
- **Won** → Customer says yes! Move to ordering
- **Lost** → Record why (price, competitor, timing) → Task to re-engage later

---

## Step 5: Order to Payment

**What happens:** The customer agreed to buy — now we deliver and get paid.

**The flow:**
```
Won Deal → Order Created → Invoice Sent → Payment Received
```

**Step by step:**
1. **Order** — Created from the won quote. Contains everything the customer bought
2. **Fulfillment** — Team starts delivering the product/service
3. **Invoice** — Generated from the order. Shows what's due and when
4. **Payment** — Customer pays. Recorded in the CRM
5. **Contract** — If it's a subscription, a contract is created with start/end dates and auto-renewal

**What the CRM tracks:**
- What's been ordered vs what's been delivered
- Which invoices are paid, overdue, or pending
- Total revenue this month, this quarter, this year

---

## Step 6: Supporting the Customer

**What happens:** The customer needs help after purchase.

**Support tickets:**
- Customer emails support@yourcompany.com
- CRM creates a ticket automatically
- Ticket is assigned to the right support agent
- SLA timer starts: "Respond within 4 hours"

**Knowledge Base:**
- Your team writes help articles and FAQs
- When a ticket comes in, the CRM suggests relevant articles
- Customer might find their answer without contacting anyone

**Ticket lifecycle:**
```
New → Open → Waiting on Customer → Resolved → Closed
```

**After resolution:**
- Customer gets a survey: "How was your experience?" (CSAT score)
- Scores are tracked to measure support quality

---

## Step 7: Contracts & Renewals

**What happens:** Keep the customer happy and renew their contract.

**For subscription businesses:**
- Contract has milestones (Go-live date, quarterly reviews)
- CRM tracks upcoming renewals automatically
- 60 days before expiry → Notification: "Contract expiring soon!"
- 30 days before → Renewal quote generated automatically
- Manager approves renewal → Contract renewed

**No more:**
- Missing renewal dates
- Losing customers because you forgot to follow up
- Manually tracking contract terms in spreadsheets

## Step 9: Automation (The CRM Works For You)

**Things the CRM does automatically so your team doesn't have to:**

| Instead of... | The CRM does this automatically |
|---------------|--------------------------------|
| Manually assigning leads | Routes leads to the right rep based on territory |
| Remembering to follow up | Creates tasks and sends reminders |
| Getting approvals in person | Sends approval request to manager's inbox |
| Sending welcome emails | Triggers email sequence when deal closes |
| Tracking contract renewals | Sends alerts 60/30 days before expiry |
| Checking for duplicates | Scans every new lead against existing records |
| Reporting numbers | Updates dashboards in real-time |
| Notifying about overdue tasks | Sends alerts to the assignee and their manager |
| Checking WhatsApp/Instagram/FB for messages | Shows all messages in one Unified Inbox; reply from CRM |

## Step 10: Unified Inbox (Multi-Channel Messaging)

**Connects WhatsApp, Instagram DM, and Facebook Messenger through one Meta Business Account.**

All customer messages appear in a single inbox inside the CRM. Your team reads and replies without switching apps. Incoming messages auto-link to the right contact — if the person is new, a contact record is created automatically. Every message and reply is logged on the contact's activity timeline.

**Key metrics:** First response time, messages per channel, resolution rate.

## The Complete Customer Journey (Summary)

```
1. A visitor fills a form on your website
   → Lead created + assigned to sales rep

2. Sales rep calls and emails
   → Lead qualified → Opportunity opened

3. Demo given, proposal sent
   → Quote created → Manager approves (if needed)

4. Customer says yes
   → Order created → Invoice sent → Payment received

5. Product delivered, contract signed
   → Customer goes live

6. Customer needs help
   → Support ticket created → Resolved → Happy customer

7. Contract renewal approaches
   → Renewal quote → Customer renews → Loyal customer

8. Customer refers a friend
   → New lead enters at Step 1 (the cycle repeats)
```

**Every step is tracked. Every interaction is logged. Nothing falls through the cracks.**
